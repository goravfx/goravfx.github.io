02 — Approach

Story first, AI second

AI has made producing images much easier. But scriptwriting, directing, lighting, editing and sound are as important as ever. A beautiful shot doesn’t make a film: what matters is whether it serves the story.

  • The AI can’t pull off the camera move I need → I block out the scene in 3D
  • I need a precise camera path → I rough out the animation in Houdini
  • A shot falls apart → I fix it with tracking, compositing or CG
  • One AI model isn’t enough → I change the approach instead of rewriting the prompt for the hundredth time

My goal isn’t to generate pretty shots. It’s to turn them into a story people watch to the end.

Concept, characters, style, music, and several thousand generations. For complex camera fly-throughs, I first built the moves in Houdini and then generated the video from them. Where precision mattered, I used tracking and compositing.

08 — Process

How I work

  1. 01

    Brief

    We figure out what the video is for and what the viewer should feel.

  2. 02

    Concept & script

    I come up with the story, the characters and the key scenes, and show you the style.

  3. 03

    Storyboard

    References, a storyboard, the first frames. For complex camera work, a rough 3D scene.

  4. 04

    Generation

    I generate images and video and keep the characters and the world consistent from shot to shot. It takes a lot of attempts.

  5. 05

    CG

    Where generation falls short: 3D, Houdini, tracking, compositing, cleanup.

  6. 06

    Edit & sound

    Editing, music, sound and color.

  7. 07

    Delivery

    Final versions for every platform and format you need.

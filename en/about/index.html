How I got here

I · KALUZHSKAYA VILLAGE

The camcorder

I grew up in Kaluzhskaya, a village in the Krasnodar region of southern Russia. When I was a kid, my uncle gave me a camcorder, and that’s where it all started.

I shot stop-motion cartoons with modeling clay and hooked the camera up to a VCR and an MP3 player to add music. I skipped school and, together with other truants like me, shot parodies of famous movies.

The results were terrible, but I didn’t care.

I just loved inventing things that didn’t exist yet.

II · THEATER

Three rejections

After school I wanted to study film directing. I didn’t get in. I tried again and was turned down again. And a third time.

So I studied theater directing instead. Theater taught me story structure, working with actors, pacing, and how an audience thinks. All of it comes in very handy now.

But I was always drawn to animation: first 2D, then Cinema 4D, 3D and VFX. My animated short “The Life of Worms” won first place for screenplay and second for animation at the Young Kinovek film festival.

I wanted to create my own worlds, not stage other people’s.

III · MOSCOW

Work by day, study by night

After university, my wife and I moved from Krasnodar to Moscow. I enrolled in the visual effects program at Scream School: Maya, Nuke, Houdini, Substance, ZBrush, modeling, lighting, animation and compositing.

Then came my first job, at the VFX studio CGF, and big feature films. After that, Unit 5, game development, and Senior and Lead roles.

That’s how a hobby became a profession.

IV · HOUDINI

Not an object, but a system

At first Houdini seemed completely incomprehensible, which is exactly why I wanted to master it. Procedural workflows, VEX, simulations, shading.

It changed the way I think: instead of building a specific object, I build a system that can produce many variations. AI works on a similar principle, just on a bigger scale.

Houdini taught me to think in systems.

V · AI

Freedom to make my own work

At first, AI was just a new toy for me: I experimented and posted tests on Instagram. Then I realized it was the tool I had been missing.

In CG you can make anything, but a complex scene means weeks of work, a team and a budget. With AI, one person can make what used to take a whole studio.

I’m not interested in churning out content. I want to make things I’d watch myself, with people who also want to try something new.

For me, AI is a chance to make my own work.

My ideal project
